Ridgeline

Flat, in the app's own logo, fonts and colors. A red line traces a ridge as you go, outlined mountains sit behind like the Alta artwork, and the background steps from night to morning. However many days were missed, it is one journey. Try 1, then 3 days.

Taken out since the last one

  • Glass, blur, glow, light rays, clouds and stars.
  • The traveler and the marking button. Less on each screen.
  • The "Day 1 of 2" label and the repeat for each day. Missing more days now means a longer, richer single journey.
  • Borrowed fonts. Headings use the site's own serif, text its own Arial.

What stays

  • The seminary pattern: find a truth, understand it, feel it, apply it.
  • Five ways to learn. Prompts that change by day. Four kinds of invitation.
  • One small flat sun in the brand red, rising behind the ridge.
  • More days missed: more ways to learn (up to three), slightly longer answers, one truth that ties it together.
