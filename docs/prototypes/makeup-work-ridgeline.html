Ridgeline

Flat, in the app's own logo, fonts and colors. A red line traces a ridge as you go, three layers of the site's own mountain artwork sit behind (randomized each time, mirrored so there is never a seam), and the background steps from night to morning. However many days were missed, it is one journey. Try 1 day, then 3 or 4 and tap the same way more than once.
