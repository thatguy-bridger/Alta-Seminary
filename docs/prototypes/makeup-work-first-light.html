First Light

Trail and Dawn combined. The scene is the progress: a traveler walks a mountain trail while the sky moves from predawn to morning. Seven screens, each a different time of day. Click through, or jump to any of them.

What changed from the first round

  • One illustrated scene instead of two separate looks: five mountain layers, stars, drifting cloud, rim light on the ridges.
  • The sun is no longer a disc. It is a soft glow and a few faint rays behind the peaks, and only appears near the end.
  • The traveler carries a lantern that fades as the sun rises. The camera follows them.

Variation built in

  • Follows the pattern seminary already uses: find a truth, understand it, feel it, apply it. Three kinds of "feel" prompt (free writing, pick a word, finish the thought) rotate by day.
  • A "listen for" invitation before you learn, different for each source. Mark moments that stand out.
  • Four kinds of closing invitation: pray, serve, share, study.
  • Five ways to learn: read, watch, listen, two conference talks, or surprise me.

At the end

  • A keepsake card with your commitment.
  • "Pass it on": a ready-made message to invite a friend.
  • What the teacher receives, tucked away unless you want to see it.
